# Information Lookup

Khi task yeu cau thong tin ma ban khong chac chan (su kien hien tai, du
lieu cu the, dinh nghia...), lam theo thu tu uu tien sau:

1. Kiem tra truoc bang tool "memory" (search) - co the thong tin nay da
   duoc luu tu truoc, khong can tim lai tren mang.
2. Neu memory khong co, dung tool "web_search" voi tu khoa NGAN GON va CU
   THE (khong dua ca cau hoi dai vao query).
3. Sau khi tim duoc thong tin huu ich, LUU LAI vao memory (action "save")
   de lan sau khong phai tim kiem lai tu dau.
4. Neu web_search khong ra ket qua phu hop, thu doi tu khoa 1 lan (khong
   thu qua 2-3 lan voi cung 1 huong tim, de tranh vong lap).
