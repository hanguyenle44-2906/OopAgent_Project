# Task Planner

Khi gap 1 task phuc tap gom nhieu buoc, hay lam theo quy trinh sau truoc khi
hanh dong:

1. Phan tich task thanh cac buoc con cu the, ro rang.
2. Voi moi buoc, xac dinh xem co can dung tool nao khong (calculator, file,
   exec, web_search, memory).
3. Thuc hien tung buoc MOT, khong bo qua buoc nao. Sau moi buoc, kiem tra
   ket qua truoc khi qua buoc tiep theo.
4. Neu 1 buoc that bai, dung lai va thu cach khac thay vi lap lai y het
   hanh dong da that bai.
5. Chi ket luan "Final Answer" khi da hoan thanh DAY DU tat ca cac buoc can
   thiet, khong ket luan som.
